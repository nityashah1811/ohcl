About OHCL

By students, for students.

OHCL is a student-led initiative promoting cricket amongst high schoolers in our community. What started as a group of players who love the game has grown into an organized league with players from five schools and more than 50 members.

Our mission is simple: give every high school student in Oregon the chance to pick up a bat, learn the game, and be part of a team — whether they've played cricket their whole life or are seeing it for the first time.

  • Grow the game

    Introduce cricket to new players and schools across Oregon at the grassroots level.

  • Compete

    Organized league matches that give high schoolers real competitive experience.

  • Lead

    Run entirely by students, building leadership and teamwork on and off the field.
